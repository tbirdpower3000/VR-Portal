🥽 VR Portal

Games and worlds for a phone in a Google Cardboard viewer, played in the browser. Pick one, press Start, turn the phone sideways and put it in the viewer.

Games

🎯 Skeet VR
Clay targets fly across in front of you. Aim with a finger gun and drop your thumb to fire, or aim by looking. 🍉 Fruit Slicer VR
Fruit is thrown towards you. Cut it with your index finger, or with your gaze.

Worlds

🪐 Planetary Modeling Atlas
The Solar System and its worlds. Press the 🥽 VR button on the map or on a globe.

For builders

The games share one VR engine (stereo view, lens fitting for the 2015 Cardboard, camera and hand finder, sound). Its code and notes are in the VR-Portal repository.

Nothing here is a medical or safety device. Clear the space around you, and stop if you feel dizzy.

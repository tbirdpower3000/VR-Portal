VR Portal v2 · step 3: floating windows

Two windows float in front of you: one of text, one that draws itself (a clock, a counter button and a slider). Under each is a bar. Look at the bar, pinch, and turn your head: the window comes with you. Let go and it stays there.

What to check on the phone

  1. Turn your head: the windows stay where they are in the room. Press Recenter (or pinch and hold on empty space) and they come back to the front.
  2. Move a window by its bar. It should keep its distance and stay facing you.
  3. Pinch the corner and turn your head right or down to make it larger; left or up, smaller.
  4. In the second window, pick the button (the count goes up) and pick a place on the slider.
  5. Close a window with the dot beside its bar. The round icons below open it again, where you left it.
  6. Exit and Start again: the windows should be where you left them.

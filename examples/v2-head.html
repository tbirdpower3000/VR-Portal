VR Portal v2 · step 1: the head

A room that follows your head. This is the first piece of the new engine: head tracking, moved out of Skeet VR into the shared engine. Nothing to aim at yet.

What to check on the phone

  1. Press Start. The phone asks to use motion. Allow it. (Refuse it once, on purpose, to see the plain message; then reload.)
  2. Put the phone in the viewer during the count of three. When it ends, the gold arrow and the word FRONT should be straight ahead of you.
  3. Turn your head. The room should stay still around you: the pillars and the words LEFT, RIGHT and BEHIND stay where they are.
  4. Face another way and press the viewer's button (or tap the screen, or Recenter). FRONT should jump to where you now face.
  5. Try it with the phone's rotation lock on, too. The picture should still come out the right way up in the viewer.

The sign in the room shows the pictures drawn each second. Back to the portal

VR Portal v2 · step 2: look and pinch

A wall of nine discs of different sizes. One is gold: look at it so the ring rests on it, then pinch your thumb and index finger together. Your hand can stay low, by your waist; it only has to be somewhere the rear camera can see it. The page counts hits and misses for each size, so the smallest comfortable target can be chosen from real numbers.

What to check on the phone

  1. The ring in the middle turns blue when your hand is seen, and gold when it rests on a disc. The disc under it lights up before you pinch.
  2. Pick the gold disc 20 or 30 times with your hand low. Which sizes are easy? Which do you miss?
  3. Pinch, then turn your head away before letting go: the pick should still go to the disc that was lit when you pinched.
  4. Put your hand down for a few seconds: the ring goes grey and fills when rested on a disc for a second.
  5. Pinch and hold on empty space for a second: straight ahead is set again. The viewer's button also picks.

VR Portal home

A home screen for a phone in a Cardboard viewer. Look at a tile and pinch: a window opens, or a card for a game with an Open button. The room around you is your own, through the camera; a dial fades it down, or swaps it for a dusk sky.

How it works

  • Aim with your head. A small ring marks the middle of your view. What it rests on lights up.
  • Line it up first. The thin line down the middle should sit between the two lenses, with the phone's edges even in the viewer.
  • Pinch to pick, thumb to index finger, with your hand low. The viewer's button does the same.
  • Pinch a window's bar and turn your head to move it. The dot closes it; the corner sizes it. While a window is open the icons step aside; the Home icon, low down, brings them back.
  • Pinch and hold on empty space for a second (or pick Recenter) to bring everything back to the front.
  • Surroundings has the dial: your room, your room dimmed, or a sky.

Nothing the camera sees is stored or sent anywhere. The phone cannot follow your eyes or where you walk, only which way your head is turned. Back to the portal
